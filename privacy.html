NestKeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

NestKeep is a home maintenance and warranty tracker published by Respawned. This policy explains what information the app handles and what you control. The short version: your home data lives on your phone, we do not sell data, we run no ads and no third-party analytics, and an account is always optional.

What NestKeep stores

On your device (default, no account): your home profile (such as home type, year built and an address label you type), maintenance tasks and history, appliance and warranty records, expenses, and receipt photos you scan or attach. This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. If you delete the app, it is gone.

Receipt scanning: receipt and nameplate photos are read using on-device text recognition. The image is processed on your phone and is never sent to us or to any server for scanning.

If you choose to create the free optional account: signing in with Google shares your name, email address and Google account ID with the app so we can identify your account. Your home data (tasks, appliances, expenses and receipt details, not full-resolution photos) is then also stored in Google Firebase (Google Cloud Firestore) under your account so it can sync across your devices and survive a phone change. Pro subscribers may also sync compressed copies of receipt images the same way. You can use every core feature without an account, and you can sign out or delete your account at any time.

Purchases: subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation of what you bought and whether it is active; we never see or store your payment card details.

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not show ads. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. We do not collect your precise location. We do not access your contacts, messages or files outside the photos you choose to scan or attach.

Service providers

The optional account, sync and purchase features rely on Google services: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Google Play Billing. Data handled by those services is governed by Google's privacy policy as our processor. If you never create an account, none of your home data leaves your phone.

Your controls

You can export your data from inside the app. You can delete your account and all synced data from inside the app (Settings, Account, Delete account), or request deletion through our account deletion page. Deleting your account removes your synced data from Firebase; data stored only on your phone is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

Children

NestKeep is intended for adult homeowners and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update this page and note the new date above.
