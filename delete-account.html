Delete your NestKeep account

You can delete your NestKeep account and all data synced to it in either of two ways.

Option 1: inside the app (instant)

  1. Open NestKeep and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and all synced data are deleted immediately.

Data stored only on your phone is separate: deleting it is done by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Option 2: email request

Send an email to ahmadameen222@gmail.com from the Google account email you used to sign in to NestKeep, with the subject "Delete my NestKeep account". We will verify the request comes from the account owner and delete the account and all synced data within 30 days, then confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Your sign-in account, your synced home profile, tasks and history, appliance and warranty records, expenses, receipt details, and any synced receipt images. Purchase records held by Google Play are governed by Google's policies and are not deleted by us. If you only used NestKeep in guest mode, you never created an account and there is nothing stored with us to delete.

See also the NestKeep Privacy Policy.
